THE FERRYMAN'S FIELD GUIDE

Two cards. Three currents.

The Keeper reveals two cards. Read the river, then place two of your five cards on different currents. Click a card, then a current. Click a placed card to recall it.

✹ Flare

Win to deal 4 hull damage. Each point of power advantage adds 1 damage, up to 3 more.

≈ Haven

Win to repair 3 hull and gain 2 guard. Guard absorbs damage and persists until used.

✦ Beacon

Win to gather 3 extra light. Every current you win also grants 1 light.

Suit circle: Cinder ✹ beats Reed ❧, Reed beats Tide ≈, Tide beats Cinder. The countering card gains +2 power. Matching suits in your two cards resonate: both gain +1, or +3 for an Echo card.

Card traits: Echo (1–2): stronger resonance. Keel (3–4): +1 in Haven. Wild (5–6): raw power. An empty opposing current has 0 power; ties wash out with no reward. Both sides resolve together.

Win: reach 14 light or break the Keeper's 24 hull. After 7 crossings, highest light wins; hull breaks a tie. An exact tie favors you. If both cross a victory threshold together, light then hull decides.

Keyboard: 1–5 select card · A/S/D choose current · Enter resolve · Esc cancel / close · ? guide · M sound. No timer. No purchases. Everything stays on this device.
